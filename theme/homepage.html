Welcome to NordFox

A hardened LibreWolf fork wrapped in the Nord colour scheme. Telemetry, Pocket, DRM, Normandy and a handful of other tracking surfaces have been stripped at the source level. The timing-attack precision floor has been tightened from 1 ms to 100 µs for all non-system callers.
